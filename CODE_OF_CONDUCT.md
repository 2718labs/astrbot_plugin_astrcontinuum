# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

English | [简体中文](#简体中文)

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone,
regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics,
gender identity and expression, experience level, education, socioeconomic status, nationality,
personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse,
inclusive, and healthy community.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- demonstrating empathy and kindness;
- respecting differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences;
- giving and accepting constructive feedback;
- taking responsibility, apologizing, and learning from mistakes;
- focusing on what is best for the community and project.

Unacceptable behavior includes:

- sexualized language or imagery, unwanted attention, or advances;
- trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks;
- public or private harassment;
- publishing another person's private information without explicit permission;
- knowingly sharing credentials, private conversation data, or sensitive vulnerability details;
- other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing acceptable behavior. They may
remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and other contributions that do not
align with this Code of Conduct, and will communicate moderation reasons when appropriate.

## Scope

This Code applies in project spaces and when an individual officially represents the project in
public spaces.

## Reporting and enforcement

For confidential reports, use the repository's
[private security advisory form](https://github.com/2718labs/astrbot_plugin_astrcontinuum/security/advisories/new)
and state that the report concerns conduct rather than a vulnerability. Do not publish private
evidence in an Issue.

Maintainers will review reports fairly, preserve the reporter's privacy as far as practical, and
may apply correction, warning, temporary restriction, or permanent removal depending on impact,
intent, and recurrence.

This Code is adapted from the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct.html).

## 简体中文

我们承诺为所有参与者提供不受骚扰的协作环境，不因年龄、身体状况、残障、族裔、性别身份、
经验、教育、经济状况、国籍、外表、种族、宗教或性取向而区别对待。

有益行为包括尊重不同观点、提供建设性反馈、承担错误并以项目和社区利益为先。骚扰、侮辱、
人身攻击、未经许可公开他人隐私、泄露凭证/私聊/未修复漏洞，以及其他不符合专业协作的行为
均不可接受。

如需保密举报，请使用
[私密安全公告表单](https://github.com/2718labs/astrbot_plugin_astrcontinuum/security/advisories/new)

是否反复发生采取纠正、警告、临时限制或永久移除等措施。

本规范改编自
